(A) a claimed witness start W > s0 of a length-2003 run fails independent gpf check
    (own trial division by primes <= 2200 AND sympy.factorint) — some W+i is 2003-smooth;
(B) a planted broken witness (one position forced 2003-smooth) is accepted by the checker;
(C) search reports a start W <= s0 (impossible by prior: L(2003)=2002 at s0 and monotonicity);

verified open lower bound to s0 + window (with checker proof that no start in the window works).
