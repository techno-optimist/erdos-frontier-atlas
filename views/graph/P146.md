### `S:deep:146` — deep audit: **WALL**, lane `wall`, board **NONE** (problems.json)

- finite object: None. A disproof requires an r-degenerate bipartite H plus a sequence of H-free graphs on arbitrarily large n with >Cn^{2−1/r} edges (an infinite construction + asymptotic lower bound); a proof requires an asymptotic upper bound over an infinite graph class. Neither is a finite witness.
- current record: Open. Best partial results: Füredi (1991) and Alon–Krivelevich–Sudakov (2003, dependent random choice) proved the one-sided special case — H bipartite with max degree ≤ r on one side ⇒ ex(n;H)=O(n^{2−1/r}).
