Theory bounds: Grimm k << log n/loglog n; Erdos-Selfridge k <= (1+o(1))log n; Ramachandra-Shorey-Tijdeman [RST75] k << (log n/loglog n)^3. Nobody has published past 1.9x10^10 in 20 years.
