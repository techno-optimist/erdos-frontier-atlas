## STATEMENT (Apache-licensed, Lean-derived)

Let $k ≥ 2$. Does there exist a prime $p$ and consecutive intervals $I_0,\dots,I_k$ such that $\prod\limits_{n{\in}I_i}n \equiv 1 \mod n$ for all $1 \le i \le k$?
