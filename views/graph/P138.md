### `S:deep:138` — deep audit: **WALL**, lane `witness-local-search`, board **READY** (problems.json)

- finite object: A 2-coloring of {1,...,N} with no monochromatic k-term AP (lower-bound witness), or a DRAT UNSAT certificate that none exists at a given N (exact value).
- current record: Exact: W(2,3)=9, W(2,4)=35 (Chvatal 1970), W(2,5)=178 (Stevens-Shantaram 1978), W(2,6)=1132 (Kouril & Paul 2008, SAT+FPGA). W(2,7) unknown. Best lower bounds: W(2,7)>=3703, W(2,8)>=11495, W(2,9)>=41265, W(2,10)>=103474 (Ahmed et al. 2014 / Kouril / Heule SAT colorings; refined by D. Monroe 2023 SLS). Asymptotic frontier: Green 2021, w(3,k) >= k^{b(k)}, b(k)=c((log k)/log log k)^{1/3}, later sharpened - algebraic, not computational.
- frontier: W(2,7) >= 3703 (Ahmed et al. 2014); W(2,6)=1132 exact (Kouril-Paul 2008)
- verifier: Poly-time exact: scan a candidate coloring for a monochromatic k-AP in O(N^2/k). Trivial and cheap - but it is a commodity every prior record-holder already runs, so no moat.
- attack: Only movable target is a lower-bound record: run farm-scale stochastic local search (DDFW/yalsat with linear weight transfer, or CDCL-with-restarts) on the CNF "2-color {1..N}, no mono k-AP" to find a valid coloring at N above the current record for some k; verify by AP scan. Rough compute: farm-weeks of restarts per candidate k, competing head-to-head against purpose-tuned specialist solvers. Exact-value W(2,7) (UNSAT+DRAT) is out of reach at N~10^4-10^5.
- applies: **witness local search** (predicate `lane-move`)
