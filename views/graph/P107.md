## STATEMENT (Apache-licensed, Lean-derived)

Let $f(n)$ be minimal such that any $f(n)$ points in $ℝ^2$, no three on a line, contain $n$ points which form the vertices of a convex $n$-gon. Prove that $f(n) = 2^{n-2} + 1$.
