- our summary: Erdős–Gallai path decomposition conjecture: every connected graph on n vertices decomposes into at most ⌈n/2⌉ edge-disjoint paths.
- witness: A single connected graph G on n vertices (an adjacency matrix) whose minimum path-decomposition number exceeds ⌈n/2⌉, together with an exhaustive proof that no decomposition into ⌈n/2⌉ paths exists (SAT UNSAT + DRAT, or a finite search tree). Concrete, but note the hard half of the witness is the refutation, not the graph.
- smallest untested case: n = 7. All connected graphs on n ≤ 6 vertices have Δ ≤ 5 and are therefore covered by Bonamy–Perrett.
