## STATEMENT (Apache-licensed, Lean-derived)

**Erdős Problem 1113.** Do there exist Sierpiński numbers that possess no finite covering set of primes? Erdős and Graham [ErGr80] conjectured that the answer is yes. A negative answer would imply that there are infinitely many Fermat primes.
