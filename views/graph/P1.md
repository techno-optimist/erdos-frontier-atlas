## STATEMENT (Apache-licensed, Lean-derived)

If $A\subseteq\{1, ..., N\}$ with $|A| = n$ is such that the subset sums $\sum_{a\in S}a$ are distinct for all $S\subseteq A$ then $$ N \gg 2 ^ n. $$
