### `S:triage:106` — triage verdict **MAYBE** (finite_handle_triage, 2026-07-26)

- our summary: Pack n squares with disjoint interiors into the unit square; f(n) = max total side length. Is f(k^2+1) = k? (Tagged FALSIFIABLE. Equivalent, by Praton [Pr08], to the full Erdos-Soifer / Campbell-Staton table f(k^2+2c+1)=k+c/k.)
- witness: A refutation is fully concrete and small: 10 squares (for k=3) given as centre, side, rotation angle -- equivalently 10 rational-coordinate quadrilaterals -- that pairwise have disjoint interiors, all sit inside the unit square, and have side-lengths summing to strictly more than 3. Because both the disjointness and the excess are STRICT, any genuine counterexample can be rationalised without loss, so a witness can always be presented in exact rational arithmetic.
- smallest untested case: k = 3, i.e. n = 10: is f(10) = 3? Exactly known values are only f(k^2)=k for all k (Cauchy-Schwarz, trivial), f(2)=1 (Erdos, k=1) and f(5)=2 (Newman, personal communication to Erdos, k=2). So k=1 and k=2 are settled and k=3 is the first open rung. OEIS A391490 (Terence Tao, Dec 2025) tabulates the CONJECTURED values k+a/k, not computed ones -- do not mistake its b-file for a frontier.
- known frontier: There is essentially none, which is the notable fact here. Baek, Koizumi and Ueoro, arXiv:2411.07274 (Nov 2024), proved the AXIS-PARALLEL variant outright: g(k^2+2c+1) = k+c/k for all -k<c<k, which determines g completely -- so every remaining degree of freedom in f is tilt. Raj Singh, arXiv:2601.22163 (Jan 2026), added only an equivalence (the conjecture for all k <=> for infinitely many k <=> convergence of sum_k (f(k^2+1)-k)); explicitly no numerics.
