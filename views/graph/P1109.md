## STATEMENT (Apache-licensed, Lean-derived)

Let $f(N)$ be the size of the largest subset $A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,N\}$ such that every $n\in A+A$ is squarefree. Estimate $f(N)$. In particular, is it true that $f(N)\leq N^{o(1)}$, or even $f(N) \leq (\log N)^{O(1)}$? This theorem formalizes the subpolynomial bound as `f(N) = O(N^ε)` for every `ε > 0`.
